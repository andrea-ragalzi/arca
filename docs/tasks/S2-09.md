# S2-09 — Occupied deck and blocked ascent

## Record

Pack Training allowed a second raptor to begin a jump onto the workbench after the first one died there. Death disabled the body collision and released its reservations while the visible body remained on top. The other predator repeatedly replanned an approach to the same occupied fixture.

`arca/enemies/raptor_jump_surface.gd` now exposes the actual top bounds. `arca/enemies/raptor.gd` treats living raptors, visible corpses, reserved landings and approaches as occupying that top. Occupancy is checked before choosing an ascent, at takeoff and again after the launch preparation. Descents remain available to a living raptor already on a top. If no ascent is available, the waiting predator watches the deck from the floor and rechecks at intervals. A gunshot from a new ground position redirects it. An ascent that makes no progress times out and releases its reservation.

The new fixture in `arca/tests/pack_arena_test.gd` checks the workbench with a live occupant, after its death, during ground watch and when a new shot redirects the surviving predator. The previous build accepted a jump onto the corpse and repeatedly moved the second actor. The previous autonomous descent regression is still part of the same suite.

The staged Godot capture `.artifacts/s2-deck-guard/deck-guard.png` in the main checkout shows the living predator holding on the floor while another body remains on the top. Pack Training passed all 80 checks under X11/Compatibility. The staged fixture and capture are automated.

The full headless gate passed 1477 checks.
