### Specification

**Owner:** Codex. **Branch:** `s1/07-sleeping-raptor`. **Depends on:** S1-02 (bake pipeline and `raptor.gd`).

**Files:** `arca/enemies/raptor.gd`, the Ferocious bake and `gameplay.tres`, `raptor_test.gd`.

**Implementation:**

- Bind `Sleep_Loop` and `Wake From Sleep`.
- Add an exported `start_mode` (`ACTIVE` default, `SLEEPING`) and a new `SLEEP` state appended to the enum.
- The raptor wakes when any of these happens:
  - it takes a hit;
  - the player fires within `wake_noise_radius` (default 12 m), via `shot_fired` on the `arca_player` group node;
  - the player stays within `wake_proximity` (default 2.5 m) for 1.0 s.
- Waking plays the wake clip, then roars into ALERT.
- A sleeping raptor is solid and shootable, but doesn't attack or rotate.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- Existing suites unchanged.
- New checks:
  - a sleeping raptor stays asleep while the player walks past at 4 m;
  - it wakes from a shot at 10 m, but not from a shot at 15 m without a hit;
  - it wakes from 1 s of proximity;
  - pause freezes the proximity timer;
  - restart restores the sleeping state;
  - killing it while asleep works and emits `exploded` once.

## Record

The raptor scene exports an opt-in sleeping start. A sleeping raptor stays solid and shootable, with no attack or turn while asleep. It wakes on an accepted hit, a shot within 12 m, or a continuous second within 2.5 m. It plays Wake before entering the existing alert roar. Pause freezes the timers, and reloading reinstantiates the starting state. Active raptors keep their previous behavior.

The Ferocious library uses the existing source bake. Godot imports the authored Sleep_Loop as Sleep. The baked sleep loop lasts 1.25 s and Wake From Sleep lasts about 1.83 s. Both retain the same root-motion treatment as the existing clips.

The first raptor run failed on the missing start mode. After implementation the raptor suite passes 95/0 and the worktree gate passes 829/0 (launcher 13, first_playable 42, raptor 95, mission 75, ambush 47, regina_player 388, economy 35, multi_predator 19 and environment_kit 115).
