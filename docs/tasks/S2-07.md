# S2-07 — Arena acquisition and response to gunfire

## Record

The Pack Training center raptor starts about 11.7 m from Regina. Its previous 10 m sight limit prevented open-floor acquisition. Cover also reduced the ordinary 13 m audible gunshot below that distance. The arena now sets 36 m of sight to cover its diagonal and a 44 m base gunshot radius before cover attenuation. The normal forward cone, ray occlusion and mission ranges still apply.

The real shot event now records where an audible shot originated, including shots that happen during flinch or recovery. The raptor advances towards that last audible location, turns towards it and enters chase sooner when sight confirms the player. Continued gunfire refreshes the acoustic memory without restarting the investigate animation on every shot. A gunshot does not put the shooter in visual memory through a wall.

Changed files are `arca/pack_arena.gd`, `arca/enemies/raptor.gd`, `arca/tests/pack_arena_test.gd` and this record. The added cases initially reproduced failure to acquire from spawn, failure to move towards a shot behind cover, and failure to close on a real shot. The final arena suite verifies all three and checks that the wall still blocks sight.

The full headless gate passed 1469 checks. Pack Training passed all 72 checks headless and on X11/Compatibility with the RTX 3050 Laptop GPU.
