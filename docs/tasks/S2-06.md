# S2-06 — Utility pack tactics, traversal and weapon feedback

## Record

The prior Pack Training build reproduced three concrete issues. At one metre past the desk approach marker, the 0.324 m free hop hit the desk face. A predator entering the space of the extended downed torso caused the visual lean to snap from roughly 1.40 to 0.42 radians because the only solid player shape stayed upright. The gun socket had both particle nodes moved directly from the robot muzzle.

- `arca/enemies/raptor_utility.gd` ranks feasible tactical actions with a per-individual seed, integer scores, minimum commitment and per-actor scheduling. `raptor_behavior.gd` exposes the traits, thresholds and weights in the Inspector. `raptor.gd` keeps the actual sighting, expiry, hearing, wounded location and execution state. Near perception is about 10 Hz, decision ranking starts at 5 Hz, and far ticks are less frequent. A recently wounded passage receives a temporary penalty. NavigationAgent3D maintains the route and the physics body follows it at the project physics rate. GEQO was absent from the repository.
- `raptor_pack.gd` and `arca/test_room.gd` provide a single encounter node for vocal emissions and approach, flank, landing and attack reservations. The same vocal cue that plays on the spatial audio player publishes the acoustic event. An eligible opposite-voice raptor responds only when the call passes its hearing test. Choosing a linked route consumes the NavigationAgent3D link event after its path update returns. This keeps native path mutation out of the signal callback. Stop, death, departure and scene cleanup release claims.
- `arca/player/regina_traversal.gd`, `arca/player/arca_player.gd` and `player/player.gd` preserve TPS input and CharacterBody3D movement. Open-floor maximum height measured 0.447 m standing and 0.447 m moving. Flight measured 0.600 s. Pack Training work surfaces are 0.76, 0.85 and 0.90 m high. The close approach now rises vertically first when the capsule needs space to clear the face. A strike, collision or newly occupied landing stops the assisted forward motion. Staged tests cover ascent, travel across the top, descent and reactions. New high raptor links require running speed above the normal standing height, with a configurable limit of 1.20 m. Pack Training verifies the three listed surfaces.
- The player keeps its upright capsule and verifies space before enabling a low auxiliary capsule under the downed torso. The auxiliary shape stays on normal collisions and leaves the scene when the player rises. Footsteps, physical landings and the provisional original effort, hit and fall cues stay tied to physical events. Existing presentation continues to use the authored rig and the gameplay AnimationTree.
- `arca/player/regina_ballistics.gd` resolves the crosshair aim with a fresh physical muzzle check. A chest-to-muzzle obstruction stops the same shot at the nearest hit. The original hit method still decides damage. Wall, metal and raptor impacts select separate particles and original spatial audio. The legacy robot retains the swept projectile. The ARCA pistol uses its own short flash and tracer and an inherited gunshot clip through AudioStreamPlayer3D.
- `arca/player/arca_player.tscn`, `arca/ui/hud.gd`, `project.godot` and `arca/mission_room.gd` wire weapon selection and both ammo states to the HUD and checkpoint. **1** selects the primary and **2** selects the six-round backup. The backup uses the same prototype model and shot tuning. Switching cancels an unfinished reload and preserves each magazine and reserve. The original ammo component still owns spending, round transfer and the no-extra-chamber convention.
- `arca/tools/replay_raptor_utility.gd` and `arca/tools/profile_raptor_pack.gd` provide headless replay and the staged rendering benchmark. The debug-only logger records once per tactical decision when `ARCA_AI_REPLAY` points to an existing directory. It records the seed, RNG cursor, observed snapshot, candidates, scores and choice. Scores are scaled integers, so JSON round trips preserve comparisons.

## Verification commands

```sh
bash scripts/check.sh
godot --headless --fixed-fps 60 -s res://arca/tests/pack_arena_test.gd
godot --headless --fixed-fps 60 -s res://arca/tests/economy_test.gd
godot --headless -s res://arca/tools/replay_raptor_utility.gd -- /absolute/path/to/raptor.jsonl
godot --headless -s res://arca/tools/replay_raptor_utility.gd -- /absolute/path/to/raptor.jsonl res://arca/enemies/velociraptor_behavior.tres
godot --display-driver x11 --rendering-method gl_compatibility --resolution 1280x720 --audio-driver Dummy --disable-vsync --profiling -s res://arca/tools/profile_raptor_pack.gd -- 3
```

Use Godot 4.5.1 Standard. In a debug build, make a writable replay directory and start Pack Training with `ARCA_AI_REPLAY` set to its absolute path. Move towards the pack to record actual decisions. Default replay checks the original rankings. Passing a behavior resource allows different scores and actions.

The recorded trace contained 25 actual decisions. Default replay matched all 25. A run with raised flank weight and caution and reduced aggression changed rankings on all 12 snapshots sampled from one actor. Pack Training and Mission Room evidence in the main checkout is under `.artifacts/s2-utility/`. Captures used simulated inputs and automatic fixtures. A human free-play session and the optional high running jump remain open. Impacts, backup weapon visuals and the downed pose are provisional.

## Current validation

The full headless gate passed 1465 checks, with 68 in Pack Training, 46 in Economy and 24 in Physical Combat. Pack Training also passed the same 68 checks under X11/Compatibility. The Economy suite drove the game input for a single shot, a burst to empty, a refused shot during reload, a miss, a near muzzle block, a partial and an interrupted reload, insufficient reserve, both weapon slots and the HUD. The pack tests exercised a real NavigationLink signal, a blocked and newly occupied landing, capsule separation, near-edge knockdown and restored input. The approximate 1.20 m upper running limit remains a tuning parameter and has no fixture in the shipped arena.

The Raptor Voice bus remains at +4 dB. A staged Mission Room recording with industrial ambience, movement, three spatial pistol shots and overlapping raptor cues peaked at -5.2 dBFS on the pre-fader Master recording with zero clipped samples. Its isolated voice stem peaked at -11.8 dBFS. Contact favored the left channel and reply favored the right.

The same six-second Pack Training fixture ran before and after under Godot 4.5.1, X11/Compatibility, Linux, NVIDIA RTX 3050 Laptop GPU. Requested window resolution was 1280×720, with VSync disabled and Dummy audio. Frame percentiles use wall-clock times measured after rendering. Python and UI automation were outside the measured runtime. AI script time excludes CharacterBody3D's final physical motion. The Godot process monitor and native profiler flag were enabled for the after benchmark.
