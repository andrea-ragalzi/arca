# S2-02 Predator senses, physical combat and pack coordination

## Brief

Build on S2-01 in the mission integration worktree. Use the existing room inheritance, raptor state machine, CharacterBody3D controllers, NavigationAgent3D, audio and source-backed animation libraries. Preserve the accepted TPS player controller outside the new combat reactions. Retain the hatch, fuse, keycard, lift, checkpoint and title path.

## Record

### P0. Environment, perception and sound

The maintenance room starts quiet even when Regina can hear a single muffled sound from the closed cargo lock. It has a pump bank, working surfaces, a CRT diagnostic desk, a service map, spare-parts shelving and a knocked chair. The corridor, office and freight gallery retain the coherent animal routes documented in S2-01.

The mission's three raptors use the Velociraptor Behavior resource. Idle and patrol perceive within a vision cone and collision-tested line of sight. Faster footsteps, gunshots and the power event provide localized stimuli, attenuated by cover. A raptor investigates a sound, watches a sighted target, and then commits to pursuit or approaches laterally. It follows a last sighting for a bounded interval and scans the area if contact is lost. A wounded raptor can temporarily withdraw. The original test-arena default remains the reference encounter.

### P1. Combat and Regina

A recognizable windup can lead to a bite, a forelimb strike, or a committed ballistic pounce. The floor and the whole capsule arc are swept before launch. Physics owns all in-world displacement. Bite, claw and pounce apply at most one hit within their respective contact windows. Cover can block the contact. Recovery follows a miss.

The crate in Security and the maintenance worktable supply authored launch and landing markers. The table has an additional downward route. Each leap rechecks clearance and a supporting landing surface. Intact gates remain physical obstacles even after the predator hears a noise on the far side.

Q makes a short collision-driven lateral dodge. A and D choose the side; Q alone defaults right. There is no invulnerability during the dash. A successful pounce knocks Regina along the impact direction and temporarily blocks movement, reload, fire and interaction. Q breaks free early and starts the recovery. The visual prototype uses the existing skin, strafe, hit pose and a temporary whole-model tilt. A dedicated contact, grab and rise animation remains to be authored. The normal player controller still owns the existing movement, camera, aim and weapon behavior.

### P2. Pack and access

A raptor with a sighting can call within a local hearing radius. Receivers investigate where the call came from and may answer. A receiver still needs to see or hear Regina itself. Nearby hunters wait or try an alternate approach while another is committed to a strike. A blocked sliding gate can trigger a pitched metallic contact cue without opening the gate. The hatch still opens through the authored power event and the freight shutter remains visibly wrecked. There is no general destruction system.
