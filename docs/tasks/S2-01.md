# S2-01 Environmental continuity

## Brief

Use the accepted Sprint 01 route to make the maintenance room, security corridor, control office and lift hall feel like functional areas of the same station. Every predator encounter has a believable entrance and a reason to remain. Physical openings, damage and optional clues communicate the sequence while leaving room for surprise.

This integration task owns the mission scene and navigation, its area scenes and builder, the area and route test additions, and the associated documentation and attribution. Preserve the maintenance puzzle, player controller, checkpoint and existing combat rules.

The maintenance scene gains overhead structure, a pump bank and supply pipes, work surfaces and transfer-lock evidence. The corridor has emergency storage and the condensate recess. The office has evacuation records and a freight status board. The hall adds the freight gallery, damaged shutter, tracks and lift hardware. The optional office log records the power dip and why the two animals are on opposite sides of the gate. No new predator controller or spawn trigger is needed.

The area check reproduced a blocked freight threshold in the baseline. It now tests the new opening using a 0.9 m radius, 2.6 m high capsule. The route suite checks that the raptor is already in the gallery at load and crosses the wrecked shutter under its normal detection and navigation. The existing cross-sector pursuit and route completion checks still pass.

Run `bash scripts/check.sh` in the worktree. Final result is 1191 checks and zero failures. Two consecutive area builds matched byte for byte.
