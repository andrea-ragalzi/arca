# S2-08 — Predator descent recovery

## Record

The Pack Training raptor previously stayed on the desk, workbench and platform when Regina stood to the side below it. The navigation target projected onto the edge of the isolated top, and every descent was screened by a rule that required the landing to be closer to Regina. Both valid descent links could therefore be discarded.

The new descent state holds an approach reservation while following the deck path. It considers both marked exits independently of immediate distance to the target. A failed capsule sweep, blocked landing or approach without progress releases the reservation and delays that exit briefly. The next eligible exit can then be tried. If both exits are blocked the raptor waits on the deck and periodically checks again. Jumps still use the CharacterBody3D and authored trajectory, with reservations released on touchdown or cancellation. Ordinary jump planning also skips links that have recently failed.

The Pack Training regression runs the actual pursuit controller from all three surfaces. It covers a direct descent, a blocked exit, and a platform with both exits occupied followed by a reopened exit. All three descents failed on the prior build.
