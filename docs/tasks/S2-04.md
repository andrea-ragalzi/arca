# S2-04 — Raptor repertoire and surface jumps

## Record

- Imported sixteen mono 48 kHz PCM cues in `arca/audio/raptor/language/`. The controller gives arena actors stable A or B voices. A contact listener must be in earshot and use the other voice. It looks towards the last audible source, then replies after the first cue plus a pause. Alert, hold, flank, threat, burst and retreat follow the actor's own states. The one shared sighting has a timestamp and can prompt investigation without satisfying the damage checks.
- `arca/enemies/raptor_performance.gd` adds original, procedural head, eye, neck, jaw, spine and tail gestures to the shipped Ferocious rig. The existing motion clips remain the base. The jump uses an additive load and landing over the existing leap clip. These poses are suitable for gameplay iteration and still need an animation polish pass.
- `arca/enemies/raptor_jump_point.gd`, `arca/enemies/raptor_jump_surface.gd` and `arca/pack_arena.*` provide a desk, workbench and platform, deck navigation, directional NavigationLink3D routes and collision. Link-layer queries identify possible crossings. The physical controller walks to the approach and reserves it, validates capsule bounds and occupancy, launches under gravity, then confirms floor contact before resuming navigation. The desk has a player access ramp on its right.
- `arca/enemies/raptor.gd` checks the best two jump approaches by distance and heading, reuses navigation goals, and staggers sensing frequency by actor and distance. Front and flank roles remain local to the pack and a flanker can resume its route after touchdown.
- `arca/tests/pack_arena_test.gd` covers stable opposite voices in a duet, WAV format, existing pack spacing and attack turns, three complete jump routes, target-led ascent, blockers and reservations. `arca/tests/predator_perception_test.gd` allows for the scheduled sampling period. `arca/tools/profile_raptor_pack.gd` profiles the rendered arena without fixed FPS.

## Validation

The full headless gate passed 1419 checks, including all 34 in the Godot pack suite. Each surface was checked for takeoff, flight, touchdown, reservation release, navigation on top and descent. A blocked arc caused the other marked approach to be picked. A player on the landing and competing raptors were tested separately. Godot rendered the desk load, flight and landing on X11/Compatibility, NVIDIA RTX 3050 Laptop GPU. The captures are in the main checkout under `.artifacts/s2-language/`. A staged PulseAudio recording in the same folder, `raptor-duet.wav`, captured the B contact and the A reply from two world positions. Peak level was -17.7 dBFS.

The profiling fixture stages a nearby fight with a stationary player for six seconds after one second of warm-up. Counts are per run, and path requests count explicit agent goals and direct NavigationServer queries. Perception samples count scheduled scans. Sight rays count player LOS tests and include combat validation. Audio uses the Dummy driver. Reported wall-frame times include GPU rendering and normal engine updates. The runs are automated and did not involve a human playthrough.
