# S2-03 — Regina field outfit and Pack Training

Apply Regina's supplied Proposal 3 design to the existing gameplay rig. Give Velociraptors distinct calls and replies, then test pack spacing and attack turns in a dedicated three-opponent room.

## Record

- `arca/pack_arena.tscn`, `arca/pack_arena.gd` and `arca/ui/title_screen.*` add **Pack Training** as a title action. The drill inherits the navigation, collision and cover of the existing test bay. Three predators start at marked ground positions in the same traversable space. Backspace restores health and all three opponents. The pause menu returns to the title.
- `arca/enemies/raptor.gd` keeps each predator's own sighting. A nearby listener that hears a call investigates its source and replies after a short delay. Range and cover affect hearing, and cover reduces voice gain. During close combat, the first attacker holds the attack window. The others look for open lateral paths. After an attack the first predator can give ground to let a nearby partner close in. Bites and slashes use the attacker’s committed heading and still require an unobstructed hit window.
- `arca/player/regina_field_outfit.gd` and `arca/player/arca_player.gd` add dark blue-black fabric, crimson trim, the S.O.R.T. sleeve mark, harness, pack, belt, arm guards, knee pads and boot details. The fitted parts follow bones on the imported rig. The suit recolor uses the original mesh, skin weights and bind poses. Face, hair and the gameplay animation library remain on their existing assets.
- `arca/tools/assets/synthesize_pack_calls.py` builds two calls, two replies and a warning growl. The synthesis blends a filtered and repitched layer from MBPL's [Crow 1](https://freesound.org/people/MBPL/sounds/668802/) under CC0. All five files regenerated byte for byte. Source checksum and regeneration command appear in [ATTRIBUTIONS.md](../../ATTRIBUTIONS.md). No film samples are used.

### Validation

- `bash scripts/check.sh` on Godot 4.5.1 Standard. 1396 headless checks passed, including 11 Pack Training checks for the title entry, three sensory predators, audible events, independent sightings, attack turns, spacing, restart, field outfit and pause-to-title.
- X11/Compatibility on NVIDIA RTX 3050 Laptop GPU. The title suite passed 8 checks and Pack Training passed 11 checks. Godot screenshots at 1280×720 are in the main checkout under `.artifacts/s2-pack/` as `pack-training.png`, `regina-front.png` and `regina-back.png`. Use `arca/tools/capture_sprint_review.gd` with `-- /absolute/output/path pack` and `--fixed-fps 60` to reproduce.
