## Record

- `default_bus_layout.tres` and `arca/enemies/raptor.tscn` add the Raptor Voice bus at +4 dB, a 5.2 m unit distance, a 32 m voice range and stronger stereo panning. The controller applies 5 dB of obstruction attenuation. Alert is trimmed up by 3 dB and threat and burst by 4 dB at the source. Regina's ARCA-only weapon playback is 8 dB lower. A Master hard limiter has a -1.2 dBFS ceiling. The original repertoire and timings remain available.
- `arca/enemies/raptor.gd` reserves strike spaces alongside flank destinations and jump landings. All close-range strikes still use the physical controller and attack window. Navigation checks the immediately occupied lane at staggered intervals and uses a short yield or sidestep when blocked by another raptor. The front has lane priority. A flanker speaks when starting the accepted maneuver or after its pending reply has finished. Roles and reservations clear on interruption or when engagement ends.
- `arca/player/regina_traversal.gd`, `arca/player/arca_player.gd`, `player/player.gd` and the marked Pack Training fixtures add collision-driven top access through normal movement and jump input. The traversal check uses camera-relative intent, the existing full capsule, overhead clearance, the width of the landing surface and the presence of other bodies. The jump follows a checked ballistic arc. Unsupported or occupied approaches use the ordinary hop.
- Regina's footsteps follow distance traveled on the floor. Jump effort, hit and fall cues are original synthesizer outputs under `arca/audio/regina/`. Body impact and landing play once at verified floor contact. A heavy hit during a rise can interrupt it. The knockdown timer waits for support, the recovery resets accumulated motion, and nearby obstacles limit the procedural fall lean. The visible model still uses a provisional lean over the current rig with the upright gameplay capsule.
- Regression coverage is in `arca/tests/pack_arena_test.gd` and `arca/tests/physical_combat_test.gd`. Documentation and provenance are in `README.md`, `docs/DESIGN.md` and `ATTRIBUTIONS.md`.

## Validation

The full headless gate passed 1443 checks, including 57 in Pack Training and 24 in Physical Combat. All 57 Pack Training checks also passed under X11/Compatibility. The test reproductions covered the old low hop falling short of the three work surfaces, an occupied or obstructed jump, a run through the narrow lane toward a predator, and a knockdown near a wall or off the desk edge. Two hits exercise the interrupted rise. The combat suite records capsule separation during an actual pounce and verifies restored input. The X11 capture fixture pressed the gameplay jump controls in Pack Training and rendered the flight, touchdown and edge knockdown. Files are under `.artifacts/s2-tps/` in the main checkout.

The staged audio capture uses Mission Room, its industrial bed, real TPS movement and three real weapon shots. Contact and reply are emitted from different positions approximately nine metres from the camera. A later close overlap includes threat and burst. Stereo placement and PCM peak levels were measured on the engine output. At the recorded positions the isolated contact favored the left channel by 4.1 dB RMS and the reply favored the right by 4.1 dB. The mixed pre-fader Master capture peaked at -1.2 dBFS with zero clipped samples. The isolated voice capture peaked at -11.8 dBFS. No human listening session or free-play run was performed.

Use F5 and **Pack Training**. Face the short side of an exercise fixture, hold movement towards it and press Space from the marked approach distance. Near the desk, leave one side of the top clear and have a pursuing raptor approach. Space still gives a low hop on the open floor.
