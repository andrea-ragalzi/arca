## Record

The raptor reuses its state and stagger signals to select positional vocalisations. Its sleep state has a continuous breath loop, and grounded motion drives three alternating footsteps. The two doors reuse a spatial servo cue with distinct denial and keycard feedback. The mission instantiates `arca/audio/mission_audio.tscn` from its scene script to keep the integration scene independent. This provides low-level ambience, the hatch alarm and a UI note cue. The alarm ends once the hatch encounter is active. Completion, restart and death silence the live world while preserving Regina's death presentation.

The standalone audio test initially failed because no mission playback was wired. After implementation: 17/0 for playback and pause, 100/0 for the imported cues, and 1002/0 for the full gameplay gate (launcher 13, first_playable 42, raptor 95, mission 82, ambush 47, regina_player 388, economy 35, multi_predator 19, environment_kit 115, areas 118 and route 48). Playback tests use the Dummy driver.

In F5, listen for ambience before power, the alarm and door servo when restoring power, the raptor cues during the hatch, and breath near the alcove. Pause while a world loop is audible and resume. Read a note, then try the lift lock with and without the card. Check that world audio falls quiet on death and victory and that gunshots do not obscure all feedback. The audible mix has not been accepted yet.
